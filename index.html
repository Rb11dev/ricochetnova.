How to Play
👆
Swipe up from the launcher to aim, then release to fire your balls. They ricochet off the walls and shatter numbered bricks. Clear rows before they reach the red danger line. Grab glowing power-up bricks for an edge.
Settings
Ricochet Nova is an original puzzle game. All art is drawn procedurally with gradients and glow effects, and every sound is synthesized live — nothing is copied from any other game.
